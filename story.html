Our story

Every bright adventure starts with a dream.

Adventure with Makeda is a home for stories that celebrate curiosity, family, imagination, and the joy of becoming exactly who you were made to be.

The girl at the center

Makeda leads with wonder.

She notices the small magic in the world: a hidden path, a new friend, a question worth asking. She is brave enough to explore and kind enough to bring others along.

Her name means greatness — and in every story she finds hers the same way. Makeda’s adventures invite children and families to celebrate strong roots, open hearts, and the bright possibilities waiting around the next corner.
